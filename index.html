Frequently Asked Questions

Who should I consult — Dr. Krishna or Dr. Roshnidevi?expand_more

For children and paediatric surgical or urological concerns, consult Dr. Krishna Induvasi. For adult or female urological concerns, consult Dr. Roshnidevi Patil.

Do you perform minimally invasive/laparoscopic surgery?expand_more

Yes — laparoscopic techniques are used in appropriately selected paediatric cases, including orchidopexy, pyeloplasty and hernia surgery.
